# PRODUCT.md — Twinverse AI

- **Name:** Twinverse AI
- **One-liner:** Personas for streaming creators — clone your identity, voice,
  and face once, and your twin deliberates, talks, and goes live as you on
  every platform at once.
- **Target user:** Content creators and busy professionals (28–45) who cannot
  be present everywhere: decisions pile up, audiences expect them live on
  five platforms, and generic AI doesn't know them or stay in character.
- **The ONE differentiator:** *Your voice. Your face. Live.* A persona that
  is provably grounded in your verified facts (inferences labeled, evidence
  scored) — not a generic avatar with a script.

## Locked hero copy (owner-approved — do not paraphrase)
> Ask once.
> Hear every side of yourself.
>
> Your twin debates your decision from three angles in parallel — then
> handles your calls and calendar like you would. Every answer scored with
> evidence. Talk to it, watch it deliberate, or put it live in your voice
> and face.

## Core features (the only 3 that own the screen)
1. **Twin Council** — one question, three lenses of you (Strategist, Skeptic,
   Counselor) in parallel, evidence-scored verdict.
2. **Talk** — a conversational clone that remembers the thread and answers
   out loud in your voice and face.
3. **Live Twin Studio** — your persona goes live (YouTube first): heads-up
   discussion points, real-time chat answers, Business/Life/Socials modes.

Foundation under all three: the **Persona** (versioned identity: verified
facts vs confidence-scored hypotheses, cloned voice + likeness, guardrails).

## Hackathon
Meta AI Developer Hackathon (Oct 26 – Nov 4, 2026). Judging lens used for
every UX decision: a judge must get the value in 30 seconds and finish the
demo in 3 minutes with zero friction (see DEMO.md).

## Non-goals (MVP)
Real multi-platform fan-out (YouTube first), phone telephony, auto-posting
to socials, cloning anyone but yourself (consent-gated), light mode.
