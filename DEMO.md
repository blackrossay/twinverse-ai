# DEMO.md — 3-minute judge demo

Prereq: `./scripts/demo.sh` (or docker compose), `MODEL_API_KEY` set (live
badge green), one dry run done. Personas auto-seed on first launch.
Backup: keep a screen recording; never present fixture output as live.

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook
- Land on `/`. Say the line: **"Your voice. Your face. Live. Clone your
  voice and likeness once — your twin speaks verdicts, talks, and goes live
  as you, on every platform at once."**
- Point at the green "live · muse-spark-1.3" badge: real model, no mocks.
- Tap Maya Chen's twin card → facts vs dashed "inferred, not verified"
  hypotheses. One line: "facts and guesses are never mixed."

## 0:20–1:30 — Core flow: Twin Council
- Tap **Ask the council** (1 tap from anywhere via nav/tab bar).
- Pick Maya Chen → tap the sample question "Should I quit my stable job to
  go freelance full-time?" → **Ask the council**.
- Session opens instantly: three lens cards running in parallel (~30–60s).
  Narrate: "one frozen identity, three minds — Strategist argues go,
  Skeptic argues stop, Counselor weighs the human cost."
- Comparison lands: read the synthesis sentence, show the three scored
  cards + deterministic code checks ("scores are model estimates with
  evidence; checks are code").

## 1:30–2:30 — Wow: the persona talks, then goes live
- Tap **Talk** → say "What should I protect this week?" out loud (mic) →
  the twin answers in character, remembers, speaks back immediately.
- Tap **Live** (or the homepage "Watch it live" card, which auto-starts a
  demo stream) → the clone is on stage with discussion points queued;
  simulated chat rolls in across platforms and the twin answers out loud,
  in its Business/Life/Socials mode.
- If a likeness is configured: tap "Speak in my voice" on a reply — cloned
  voice; "Avatar video" — its face speaking the words.

## 2:30–3:00 — Close
- Show the Publish panel: stream key masked server-side, one click to push
  to YouTube. "Today it deliberates, talks, and streams as you. One
  identity — your voice, your face, live everywhere, under your rules."

## If something fails live
Say so, show the honest error state, cut to the backup recording.
Fixture output is labeled in-band — point at the label, it's a feature.
Reset between runs: footer **Reset demo** (wipes data, reseeds personas).
